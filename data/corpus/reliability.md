# Nimbus Reliability and Support.

Nimbus targets 99.95 percent monthly availability for Enterprise organizations. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance is excluded from the availability calculation. A Priority One incident means the production service is unavailable to most users or customer data is at immediate risk.

The on-call engineer acknowledges Priority One incidents within 15 minutes. Nimbus publishes an initial customer update within 30 minutes of confirming a Priority One incident. Subsequent updates are posted every 60 minutes until mitigation. A written incident report is delivered within five business days after resolution.

Enterprise support is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Team support operates from 09:00 to 17:00 Eastern Time on business days. Starter customers use community support. Status updates are published at status.nimbus.example, and customers can subscribe by email or webhook.

# Nimbus Plans and Billing.

Nimbus offers Starter, Team, and Enterprise plans. The Starter plan includes three projects and 10 gigabytes of storage. The Team plan includes unlimited projects and 250 gigabytes of storage. Enterprise storage limits are negotiated in the order form.

Subscription upgrades take effect immediately and the remaining billing period is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Nimbus does not issue refunds for unused time after a customer cancels a monthly subscription. Annual subscriptions may be refunded within 14 calendar days of the initial purchase.

Usage above the included storage allowance is billed at 12 cents per gigabyte-month. Billing administrators receive an email when an organization reaches 80 percent and 100 percent of its storage allowance. Invoices are issued in US dollars on the first day of each billing cycle. Customers must contact support within 30 days to dispute an invoice.
